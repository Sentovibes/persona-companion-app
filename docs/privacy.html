Privacy Policy for Persona Companion

Effective Date: September 1, 2026

Overview

Persona Companion ("we", "our", or "the app") is designed as an offline reference and utility guide for fans of the Persona video game series. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

We do not collect, sell, or transmit any personal identifiable information (PII). The app operates primarily offline on your device.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
  • We do not collect financial, payment, or banking information.
  • We do not track precise or approximate GPS location.
  • We do not create user profiles or track your identity across apps or websites.

Permissions & On-Device Features

The app requests the following Android permissions strictly for local app functionality:

📷 Camera Permission (Optional / AI Scanner)

Used exclusively for the on-device AI Screen Scanner & OCR feature to recognize in-game text and enemies. All optical character recognition (OCR) and machine learning processing occurs 100% locally on your device via Google ML Kit / TensorFlow Lite. No images, video frames, or scanner data are ever uploaded or transmitted over the internet.

🌐 Internet Access

Used solely to download optional high-definition image asset packs from GitHub Releases / CDN and to power the optional local Wi-Fi casting feature (displaying guides on your local TV or secondary screen via local HTTP/WebSocket server). We do not send your data to external analytics servers.

🔔 Notifications & Foreground Service

Used strictly to keep the local display cast server or active asset download service running smoothly when placed in the background.

Data Storage & Local Preferences

All user data — including saved favorites, tracked fusion checklists, social link notes, and app preferences — is stored strictly locally on your device using Android Room Database and SharedPreferences. You can clear this data at any time through your device's Application Settings ("Clear Data" / "Clear Cache") or by uninstalling the application.

Third-Party Services & Advertising

Persona Companion does not contain advertising (no AdMob or third-party ad networks) and contains no third-party tracking or behavioral analytics SDKs.

Children's Privacy

Persona Companion does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. Because our application does not collect any personal data whatsoever, it is fully compliant with COPPA and Google Play Family Policy requirements.
