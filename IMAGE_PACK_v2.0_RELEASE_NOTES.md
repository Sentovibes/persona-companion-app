# Persona Companion Image Pack — v2.0 Release Notes

Release repository: https://github.com/Sentovibes/persona-companion-images/releases/tag/v2.0  
Target release asset: `images.zip`

---

## Overview

Image Pack v2.0 is the definitive, **100.0% complete high-resolution artwork package** for the Persona Companion App. It delivers complete visual asset coverage for every Persona and Enemy across all 7 supported games (Persona 3 FES, Persona 3 Portable, Persona 3 Reload, Persona 4, Persona 4 Golden, Persona 5, and Persona 5 Royal).

---

## What's New in v2.0

### 1. 100.0% Artwork Completion
- **Personas (320 / 320 — 100% Coverage)**:
  - Added full high-resolution transparent renders for all remaining Personas:
    - `Abaddon`
    - `Ananta`
    - `Anat`
    - `Koropokkuru`
  - Zero missing Personas across the entire compendium.
- **Enemies & Bosses (610 Unique Models — 100% Coverage)**:
  - Added authentic artwork and battle sprites for all remaining enemies and dungeon bosses:
    - `Ill-fated Maya` (P3 Tartarus rare shadow)
    - `Tank-Form Shadow` (P3/P3R Chariot & Justice combined August boss)
    - `Craven Venoms` (P3R Harabah 179F Gatekeeper)
    - `Kunino-sagiri` (P4/P4G story boss)
    - `Ameno-sagiri` (P4/P4G story boss)
    - `Kusumi-no-Okami` (P4G Hollow Forest boss)
    - `Abyssal King of Avarice` (P5/P5R Shadow Okumura boss)
    - `Justine & Caroline` (P5/P5R Velvet Room dual boss)

### 2. Streamlined Archive & Clean Footprint
- **Size Reduction**: Reduced total package size from ~1.3 GB down to **1.02 GB (1,026 MB)** by purging orphaned legacy backups and unneeded temporary assets.
- **File Count**: Exactly **930 active PNG assets** matching the production compendium 1-to-1.

### 3. App Compatibility
- Fully compatible with **Persona Companion App v7.2.0+**.
- Built for automatic in-app extraction via `ImageDownloadManager` into internal app storage (`context.filesDir/images/`).
- Can also be downloaded manually and imported via **Settings -> Import images.zip**.

## How to Install / Use

### In the Android App:
1. Open the **Persona Companion App**.
2. Go to **Settings -> Images**.
3. If not yet downloaded, tap **Download Images** to fetch Image Pack v2.0 automatically.
4. Alternatively, if downloaded manually in your browser, tap **Import images.zip** and select the file from your Downloads folder.
